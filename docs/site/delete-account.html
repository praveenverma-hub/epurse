Delete Your Account & Data

ePurse has no backend server — everything it knows about you lives in one of two places: on your device, or in your own Google Drive. This page covers deleting both, whether or not you still have the app installed.

There is nothing on our servers to delete, because we don't have a server. ePurse never uploads your transactions, messages, or contacts anywhere. Deleting your data means clearing it from your device and, if you used backup, from your own Drive — steps only you can take, which is exactly what this page walks through.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest, if you still have it installed)

Settings → Logout → Delete Account & Data

Open ePurse, go to Settings, and tap Delete Account & Data below Logout. Confirm once. This immediately:

  • Erases every transaction, account, budget, goal, and reminder stored on your device
  • Signs you out and revokes ePurse's access to your Google account
  • Returns the app to a fresh, signed-out state

This does not delete a Drive backup you made earlier — see Option 2 for that, since it lives in your own Drive, not ours.

Option 2 — Delete your Drive backup

Only relevant if you ever used ePurse's optional backup. It writes one file to your own Google Drive — we never see it unencrypted, and we can't delete it for you remotely.

Find and remove it yourself
  1. Open drive.google.com and sign in with the account you used in ePurse
  2. Search for epurse-backup
  3. Select the file(s) and delete them (and empty Trash, if you want it gone immediately rather than after 30 days)

Option 3 — Don't have the app anymore?

Revoke ePurse's access from your Google account

This is the one step that works with no app installed. It immediately cuts off anything ePurse could otherwise do with your Google sign-in.

  1. Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions
  2. Find ePurse and select Remove Access

Then follow Option 2 above to remove any backup file left in your Drive. On-device data from an uninstalled copy of the app is already gone with the uninstall — Android does not keep app data around afterward.

Or, email us directly

If you'd rather have us confirm it for you: support@epurse.co.in. We'll reply with the exact steps above for your situation — there's no separate request form because there's no account record on our side to look up.

What is retained, and for how long

SMS message textNever leaves your device. The parsed transaction (merchant, amount, date) is kept in the app's normal retention window; the raw message text itself is dropped after that window regardless of whether you delete your account.
ContactsRead only to label people in splits/lending — never stored anywhere but your device, gone the moment you delete your account or uninstall.
LocationA coarse city label only, attached to individual transactions on your device — never coordinates, never uploaded.
Google account identityYour name/email/photo, held only long enough to show you're signed in. Deleted on sign-out, deletion, or if you revoke access via Google.
Drive backupAn encrypted file in your own Drive, containing parsed values only (never raw SMS). Retained until you delete it — see Option 2.
On any ePurse serverNothing. There is no backend.
